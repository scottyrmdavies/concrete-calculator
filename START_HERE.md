## What Just Happened?

✅ **All your calculators now support UK AND US markets**

### For UK Users:
- Enter: `2.5` (meters)
- Get: Results in m², m³, tonnes
- See: "Timber", "Plasterboard", "Skip"

### For US Users:
- Enter: `8' 2"` (feet-inches) OR `8.17` (feet)
- Get: Results in sq ft, cu ft, lbs
- See: "Lumber", "Drywall", "Dumpster"
- Materials: Actual lumber dimensions (2x4 = 1.5" x 3.5")

## Key Features

🎯 **Smart Input:**
