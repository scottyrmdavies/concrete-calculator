🔍 How Regional Settings Work:

  • UK (Metric): Enter values as decimals (e.g., 2.5 meters)
  • US (Imperial): Enter feet and inches (e.g., 8' 2" or 8 2)
  • Vocabulary: Terms change automatically (Timber/Lumber, Plasterboard/Drywall)
  • Standards: Material sizes use regional specifications
  • US Lumber: Calculations use ACTUAL dimensions (e.g., 2x4 = 1.5" x 3.5"), not nominal
